# Philosophy of AI — Seminar Laboratories

Five laboratories for the seminar *Philosophy of Artificial Intelligence* (TUM, Winter Semester 2026/27, Prof. Stefania Centrone, assistant Marcos Silveira). One laboratory per session from Session 2 to Session 6. Each lab is the 15:30 to 17:00 slot, runs on Google Colab from GitHub, and is written for people who know how to code but are not programmers. The goal is insight, not code.

Source programme: `planning/archive/programme.md`.
